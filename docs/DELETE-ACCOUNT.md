# Delete your VideoCallLive account

This page explains how to request deletion of your VideoCallLive account and associated information.

## Delete from inside the app

After the privacy update is released, open VideoCallLive and go to:

**My Profile → Delete Account → confirm “Delete permanently”.**

The app will ask you to confirm because deletion cannot be undone. Once the request completes, our backend deletes the Firebase Authentication account and the account's profile and private creator-application data, stored profile/selfie files, app-side payment-order records, support tickets, chats and call records stored by VideoCallLive.

Deleting a shared chat or call record removes that record from the VideoCallLive service for the participants involved. Other users may have separately copied or recorded content outside the app; we cannot delete those external copies.
